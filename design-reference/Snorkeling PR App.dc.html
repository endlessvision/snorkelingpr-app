RADII & SPACING
8-pt grid · radii 8/14/20/full · soft shadows only, never harsh. Generous touch targets (≥44px) for kids & gloved hands.
Motion principles
Every animation should feel like water. Scrolling feels like diving; the interface dissolves and the ocean becomes the experience.
🌊
Scroll = descent
Water darkens, sunlight fades, rarer life appears as you go deeper. Parallax layers drift at different speeds.
🫧
Bubbles on every win
Taps, unlocks & refresh release rising bubbles. Discoveries burst with a gentle caustic shimmer.
🐢
Everything floats & sways
Cards bob, creatures sway. Easing is always soft — cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1), the “tide” curve. No hard stops.
Tide easing Caustic light Haptics on unlock
Phased roadmap
Revenue first, love second, scale third. Every phase earns its place.
PHASE 1
Book & wow
Ocean home, direct booking, meet-your-crew, before/during/after basics. Drives revenue from day one.
PHASE 2
Passport & rewards
Ocean Passport, stamps, Ocean Coins, species collection. Turns one trip into return visits.
PHASE 3
Community
Adventure journals, family challenges, referrals, shareable recaps. Referrals lower cost of acquisition.
PHASE 4
Deep gamification
Rare discoveries, seasonal events, treasure bubbles, premium membership. Lifts engagement & LTV.
PHASE 5
Marketplace
Charters, photography, partners, merch, conservation donations. New revenue beyond tours.
